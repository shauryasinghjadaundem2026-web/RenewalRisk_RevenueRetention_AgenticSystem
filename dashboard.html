Routing policy

Retention Playbook v1.0

Escalate to a human

Risk ≥ 70 and ARR ≥ $15,000, or Enterprise tier with Medium+ risk, or the top option needs a discount over 10% or costs more than $1,000. (Playbook Clause 1)

Act autonomously

Everything else, capped at a 10% discount or $1,000 of outreach spend per account per cycle, fully logged. (Clause 2)

Reactivation guardrail

Accounts with a past churn-and-return pattern get one further autonomous attempt, then mandatory escalation. (Clause 6)

Audit & override

Every decision — autonomous or human — is logged with its reasoning trace and the clause it relied on. A human can override any recommendation. (Clause 7)

How the numbers are built

methodology & honesty notes

Risk score fitted

Logistic regression trained on the account's own churn outcome (— base rate). Holdout AUC — — a real but moderate lift over chance; treat scores as triage, not certainty.

CSM / exec / feature-enablement uplift model-simulated

Each option perturbs the features that intervention plausibly changes (satisfaction, escalation, usage) and re-scores with the same trained model. This is a counterfactual simulation, not a randomized trial.

Discount uplift assumed

RavenStack has no discount history to fit on. Uses an externally benchmarked SaaS elasticity (0.6pp churn-probability reduction per 1% discount, capped at 20pp) — Playbook Clause 5. Replace with an internal A/B figure once available.

Leakage discipline

Churn reason, refund amount, and subscription end-date are outcome-only fields and are excluded from the model. Only signals visible while an account is still active are used.

Data source: RavenStack B2B SaaS dataset (accounts, subscriptions, churn events, support tickets, feature usage). Kaggle itself is not reachable from this build environment's network policy, so this dataset — a structured, realistic B2B SaaS book of business built for renewal-risk / survival analysis — was used as the real-data substitute. All figures on this page are computed from that data, not fabricated for display.
